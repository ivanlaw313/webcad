Roboto is a vector (TrueType) font with proper glyph counters (interior holes),
used here for parametric 3D text (replicad drawText, fontFamily 'cad'). Apache-2.0
permits embedding, bundling, modification, and commercial use. No GPL/AGPL.

Source: @expo-google-fonts/roboto (Roboto_400Regular.ttf), originally from Google Fonts.
Replaces the previous kenpixel.ttf (a CC0 PIXEL font that has no counters, so
letters like O/A/B/e/8 could not produce interior holes when extruded).
